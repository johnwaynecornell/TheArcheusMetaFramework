        <div><hr></div><h2><strong>1. Philosophy of the AMF</strong></h2>
        <p>The <strong>AMF</strong> is built on three guiding principles:</p>
        <h3><strong>Transparency</strong></h3>
        <p>Understanding the system fully is key to leveraging its potential. The AMF prioritizes clear, accessible structure and documentation to empower users.</p>
        <h3><strong>Adaptability</strong></h3>
        <p>Dynamic systems demand flexible tools. The AMF’s layered architecture ensures relevance across changing contexts.</p>
        <h3><strong>Synergy</strong></h3>
        <p>By integrating logic, context, and governance, the AMF creates emergent opportunities greater than the sum of its parts.</p>
        <h4><strong>"An Ounce of Foresight..."</strong></h4>
        <p>The AMF embraces predictive insight as a core tenet. Rather than relying solely on hindsight, it equips users to anticipate change and align systems proactively.</p>
