        <p>While <strong>feedback loops enhance cognition</strong>, they must be:</p>
        <ol>
            <li><strong>Directed</strong> (Avoiding infinite feedback recursion).</li>
            <li><strong>Validated</strong> (MCF ensures coherent transformation).</li>

            </ol>
        <h3><strong>4.1. Preventing Echo Chambers</strong></h3>
        <ul>
            <li><strong>Echo chambers</strong> occur when feedback loops <strong>reinforce bias rather than refine clarity</strong>.</li>
            <li>AMF-AllSpark <strong>introduces counterbalancing edges</strong> to <strong>challenge weak thought structures</strong>.</li>
            </ul>
        <p><strong>Solution:</strong></p>
        <ul>
            <li>Introduce <strong>divergent feedback pathways</strong>.</li>
            <li>Enforce <strong>cross-domain connections</strong> to <strong>expand symbolic perspectives</strong>.</li>
            </ul>
        <h3><strong>4.2. Ensuring Thought Adaptability</strong></h3>
        <ul>
            <li><strong>If thought structures become rigid, innovation stalls.</strong></li>
            <li>MCF introduces <strong>controlled perturbations</strong> to test <strong>adaptive robustness</strong>.</li>
            </ul>
        <p><strong>Example:</strong></p>
        <ul>
            <li>Thought <strong>A → B → C</strong> stabilizes.</li>

            </ul>
