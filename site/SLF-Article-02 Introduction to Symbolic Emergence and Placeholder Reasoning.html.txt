        <h4>What Is a Symbolic Placeholder?</h4>
        <p>A <strong>symbolic placeholder</strong> is a symbolic entity that has not yet been named — but is already meaningful.</p>
        <p>It might look like this: <code>&sect;sym_001</code>, <code>&sect;user_002</code>, or <code>&sect;notion_003</code>.</p>
        <p>Each is a stand-in for:</p>
        <ul>
            <li>
                <p>A symbol in formation</p>
                </li>
            <li>
                <p>A concept under reflection</p>
                </li>
            <li>
                <p>A structure held open for evolution</p>
                </li>
            </ul>
        <p>Unlike a variable in programming, a symbolic placeholder is not just a bucket — it is a <strong>living marker</strong> of symbolic emergence.</p>
        <hr>
        <h4>Why Use Them?</h4>
        <p>Because <strong>naming too early fixes meaning</strong>.</p>
        <p>Symbolic placeholders allow us to:</p>
        <ul>
            <li>
                <p>Hold open a symbolic possibility</p>
                </li>
            <li>
                <p>Collaborate on ideas that are still taking shape</p>
                </li>
            <li>
                <p>Observe symbolic structures as they develop meaning over time</p>
                </li>
            </ul>
        <p>They are tools of <strong>respectful delay</strong> — giving cognition room to unfold before collapsing into syntax.</p>
