  <h1>LOM-01: Lattice of Meaning · Canonical Sigma Spec</h1>

  <p>
    This page publishes the canonical <code>LatticeOfMeaning.sig</code> for the
    Archeus Meta-Framework. The Lattice of Meaning is the structural spine that
    organizes symbolic cognition across three core axes:
    <strong>Meta</strong> (why, principle),
    <strong>Form</strong> (what, structure),
    and <strong>Function</strong> (how, effect).
  </p>

  <p>
    <strong>Foundational symbolic sources:</strong> LOM inherits its Meta/Form/Function
    grammar from <code>SLF-MOD-03.sig</code>. Contextual operator constructions may be
    imported from <code>SLF-OP-PLACEMENT-00.sig</code> and its specialized
    <code>SLF-MOD-03-ANNEX-01.sig</code> without promoting those locally declared
    operators into the universal SLF vocabulary.
  </p>

  <p>
    The specification is organized into five main parts:
  </p>

  <ul>
    <li>
      <strong>Plan</strong> &mdash; defines the guiding axes and traversal patterns
      for meaning (e.g., Potential ↔ Identity ↔ Change, Expression ↔ Relation ↔ Purpose).
    </li>
    <li>
      <strong>TruthsOfReason</strong> &mdash; captures <em>Why / What / How</em> of
      reasoning as triadic structures, grounding motivation, structure, and operation.
    </li>
    <li>
      <strong>Place</strong> &mdash; anchors meaning in a triadic coordinate system,
      describing how Potential, Identity, and Change are located within the lattice.
    </li>
    <li>
      <strong>Modules</strong> &mdash; a set of triadic, Meta/Form/Function modules
      such as ExpressionFlow, SymbolicCoordination, MetaSymbolResolution,
      ExpressionTyping, SymbolicIndexing, RelationalSignature, TriadicReasoning,
      FrameworkMapping, and VectoralAnalogy.
    </li>
    <li>
      <strong>Annex</strong> &mdash; supporting clauses, notes, and integration
      commentary (including FAR and cross-model exchange notes).
    </li>
  </ul>

  <p>
    Within the broader Archeus Meta-Framework, the Lattice of Meaning acts as a
    bridge between:
    <strong>SLF</strong> (logic-space: operators, structures, expressions),
    <strong>ARF</strong> (context-space: signals, priorities, paths),
    and <strong>MCF</strong> (governance-space: reflection, intent, oversight),
    all under the ambient Information Continuity Field (Σ<sub>Info</sub>).
  </p>

  <p>
    The module <code>Mod.VectoralAnalogy</code>, co-authored through a FAR Practitioner
    exchange with an external model (Gemini), extends the Lattice to cover
    hybrid symbolic–vectoral reasoning. It formalizes analogical structure
    (A : B :: C : D) in SLF, while providing an ARF-compatible mechanism that
    operates over high-dimensional embedding space. This makes the Lattice
    natively interoperable with modern language-model style semantics.
  </p>

  <p>
    The Sigma source below is intended to be both human-readable and
    machine-actionable: FAR practitioners, agents, and models can treat it
    as a reference implementation of the Lattice, or as a seed for emulation
    and extension.
  </p>
