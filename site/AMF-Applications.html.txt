        <p><strong>Problem</strong>: Reduce congestion and improve traffic efficiency in a metropolitan area.</p>
        <p><strong>Application</strong>:</p>
        <ul>
            <li>
                <p>Use symbolic logic to model traffic signals:</p>
                <pre><code>L&nbsp;=&nbsp;(R&nbsp;&rarr;&nbsp;G)&nbsp;&and;&nbsp;(B&nbsp;&rarr;&nbsp;R)</code></pre>
                <p>where R = Red, G = Green, B = Blue signals.</p>
                </li>
            <li>
                <p>Apply transformation rules to balance flow dynamically.</p>
                </li>
            </ul>
        <p><strong>Outcome</strong>:</p>
        <ul>
            <li>
                <p>Improved traffic flow.</p>
                </li>

            </ul>
