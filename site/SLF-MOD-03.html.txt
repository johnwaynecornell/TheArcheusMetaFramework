        <p><strong>SLF-MOD-03: Mod.MetaFormFunction</strong></p>
        <hr>
        <p><strong>Symbolic Module</strong>: <code>Mod.MetaFormFunction</code></p>
        <p><strong>Canonical Source</strong>: <code>SLF-MOD-03.sig</code><br>

        <p><strong>Symbolic Core</strong>: <code>Meta&nbsp;&harr;&nbsp;Form&nbsp;&harr;&nbsp;Function</code></p>
        <p><strong>Purpose</strong>: To provide a triadic symbolic structure for aligning intention, representation, and execution across symbolic, cognitive, physical, and design systems. This pattern applies universally across realms (Qualitative, Quantitative, Hybrid) and facilitates interpretation, modeling, and transformation in both abstract and applied reasoning.</p>
        <hr>
        <h3>1. Triadic Structure: Meta ↔ Form ↔ Function</h3>
        <ul>
            <li>
                <p><strong>Meta</strong>: That which governs, defines, or provides intent.</p>
                </li>
            <li>
                <p><strong>Form</strong>: The structural or symbolic embodiment.</p>
                </li>
            <li>
                <p><strong>Function</strong>: The operation or realization in context.</p>
                </li>
            </ul>
        <p>This module posits that:</p>
        <pre><code>Meta&nbsp;defines&nbsp;Form
Form&nbsp;enables&nbsp;Function
Function&nbsp;expresses&nbsp;Meta
        </code></pre>
        <p>Each node in the triad completes a reflective loop.</p>
        <hr>
        <h3>2. Realms of Application</h3>

            <li>
                <p><strong>Nature</strong>: interpretive, pattern-based, symbolic</p>
                </li>
            </ul>

        <ul>
            <li>
                <p><strong>Quantifier</strong>: ∃ (There exists)</p>
                </li>
            <li>
                <p><strong>Axes</strong>: time, frequency, output, magnitude, ...</p>
                </li>
            <li>
                <p><strong>Nature</strong>: measurable, functional, instance-based</p>
                </li>
            </ul>

                <p><strong>Interaction</strong>: Qualitative ∩ Quantitative</p>

                <p><strong>Nature</strong>: Resonant, dual-mode, symbolic execution</p>
                </li>
            <li>
                <p><strong>Application</strong>: cognition modeling, symbolic simulation, MSE structures</p>
                </li>
            </ul>
